The owner drove an existing main-branch build in the `korikone-live` checkout, with real stores and ChatGPT connected. Codex observed window snapshots and read source; it did not submit AI requests, search retailers, change products or transfer a list. The exact build commit and operation durations were not captured. These findings describe observed results and owner reports, not established root causes. Implementation cards and statuses are in [v0.10.0 B2](tasks/0.10.0.md#b2-fixes-from-the-12-october-owner-playtest).

## Withdrawn finding and successful behavior

The first storefront list showed about EUR 41.33. After the owner cleared its contents and repeated the transfer, they reported correct products and EUR 14.08. The owner explicitly withdrew the earlier storefront-total discrepancy. Do not open a wrong-total or duplicate-write bug from that observation. The slow transfer and confirmation placement remain findings. Prior unrelated storefront contents are a possible explanation, not a reconciled fact.

The initial request completed, a potato alternative was selected, and the repeat S-kaupat handoff worked. Development fixtures must reproduce the failure boundaries without using the owner's accounts; these successes do not establish acceptance of every matching or transfer case.

## Follow-up: useful default proposals and duplicate progress

After the session, the owner identified the main usability goal: a vague description such as "uunimakkaraa" should produce a concrete proposal with real store products, so the shopper can inspect it and proceed quickly. Unwanted choices can be changed directly or through the existing composer. The owner chose a simple complete meal with a side by default, unless otherwise specified. B2.13 establishes that proposal contract; B2.14 presents assumptions and correction paths. This is new planned behavior, not an observed successful match or permission to invent product/price facts.

A later supplied screenshot shows the note `Uunimakkaraa, perunamuusia, poronkäristystä` during interpretation. It shows a global "Working… / Cancel" and a composer "Interpreting your request… / Cancel list update" simultaneously. B2.15 consolidates those surfaces while preserving visible progress when the composer is off-screen. The image was reference evidence only; its outcome was not captured, and no local screenshot is committed.

## Open product question

The owner suggested a future editable date for shopping on another day. The existing chip represents today, not a retailer pickup reservation or the F9 meal calendar. Clarify whether a future feature means a local shopping target date, meal scheduling, or an actual retailer slot. B2.2 fixes the misleading appearance now; it does not authorize reserving slots, changing delivery dates, or a new calendar schema.

## Work boundaries

Later owner clarification on 12 October: default to two actions, generate and transfer/open, without another routine confirmation. B2.16 supersedes this report's original instruction to retain mandatory review; B2.9 now places inline transfer details and progress by the list, with review only when explicitly enabled. B2.17/B2.18 add the distinctive "Taio ostoslista" / "Taio muutokset" primary action and progress animations; B2.19 exposes optional Advanced review. Real product/quantity/account validation, duplicate-safe journal verification and manual storefront checkout remain. This changes planned behavior, not the facts observed in the session.

Keep existing card statuses and implementation evidence. B2 is new planned work, not a claim that earlier fixture checks failed. Reuse the provider-neutral inference seam, row-correction, matching, package confirmation and transfer journal; do not build parallel systems. Strong cards publish exact contracts and deterministic fixtures before Simple handoff. Use focused checks only; the full release flow is reserved for release preparation under AGENTS.md. Any later live acceptance needs a separate bounded request, with expected AI/retailer usage stated.
