# Product decisions

Receipt-learning review, 10 October 2026 after v0.6.0 publication: adopt [receipt-learning.md](receipt-learning.md) for v0.9.0 and provider-neutral AI foundations for v0.7.0. ChatGPT stays default; task schemas/validation/call policy are separate from provider transport/auth/capabilities, with fake alternative-provider coverage before live local/other adapters. Existing F21 local-runtime scope remains later.

The owner chose a small first-import opt-in for sanitized ambiguous-label enrichment, also changeable in Settings, and sanitized evidence by default with optional local retention of originals. Local parsing is the default. The adopted plan preserves unknown money/quantities, unsupported group-discount allocations and non-food purchase facts, while excluding non-food from meal suggestions. Purchases remain observations until explicit Remember; ambiguous cross-source matches require review and transfers never establish actual spending. One enrichment call per explicit action, no automatic chain or cloud fallback. Original owner receipts stay private under ignored scratch/receipts and are never published.

Current release sequencing is in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md); implementation status lives in [version task lists](tasks/README.md). Dated decisions below retain their original context; completed implementation supersedes old investigation wording. The [shopping experience plan](shopping-experience.md) specifies the accepted 10 October additions.

## Agreed 12 October 2026

Price-ranking update: Settings > Stores gets one shared comparison-basis setting for both chains: lowest unit price (default) or lowest total cost for enough whole packs. The owner confirmed it governs both initial automatic product selection and alternative recommendations. This supersedes the earlier unconditional whole-pack-total default. Suitability, explicit requirements, exclusions, remembered rules and the existing brand policy still come before price; retain deliberate product choices. Unit price uses comparable verified content, such as EUR/kg, EUR/l or EUR/individual piece, not package sticker price or incomparable units. Refundable deposits stay in payable totals rather than the unit-value metric. Conditional discounts count only with established eligibility and qualifying purchase quantities; unknown content/price cannot claim best unit value. In unit mode, a recommendation may increase the amount paid: show both unit price and cost for the required whole packs, and label it "Lower unit price" rather than implying a checkout saving. Totals, budget information, retailer writes and store comparisons continue to use actual purchased quantities and payable costs. B2.22 establishes the shared contract and B2.23 delivers the option and recommendation presentation. Setting changes make no AI/retailer call; automatic selection applies on the next explicit generation/repricing, while cached alternative suggestions can refresh locally.

Stores presentation update: the owner supplied a screenshot of redundant Store connection/active-store/Store chains headings above the existing richer chain cards. Give the primary button a distinctive restrained treatment, with an immediate busy transition and meaningful progress animation while inference/search is running. Keep text stages, cancellation and actual validated previews; no invented progress percentages or generated product/price claims. Respect reduced motion, contrast and keyboard focus. B2.17/B2.18 implement this presentation using B2.15's single visible progress surface and the provider-neutral operation state. ChatGPT remains the default, but the button must also make sense with a future different provider.
