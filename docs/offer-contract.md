## K-Ruoka personal fields

The response has offers. Each entry can contain localizedTitle.finnish, pricing.price in euros, pricing.unit.fi, pricing.discountPercentage, normalPricing.price, remainingQuantity, validUntil, and products[].product with ean, localizedName.finnish and isAvailable. Missing availability stays unknown, not true. Product variants are alternatives for the same offer; do not require buying every listed EAN.

A price can cover a bundle. For the audited source example, a price described by unit.fi equal to 3 kpl buys three pieces, not one. Recognize only explicit bounded integer kpl quantities or a single kg/l basis; preserve the original unit label and unknown basis for other strings. Do not divide an offer price by a guessed bundle size, derive a pack size, or use a percentage/range to invent an effective price. normalPricing.price has no proven matching basis and is retained as reference-only cents, not a comparable saving or per-item price.

remainingQuantity counts remaining offer redemptions, not individual pieces. Zero is exhausted and omitted even with a future expiry. Missing count stays unknown. Explicit past expiry is omitted. A date-only expiry lasts through that Finnish calendar day; an ISO timestamp expires at its exact instant. Missing expiry stays conditional and labelled unknown. Source observations that offers were already on a card do not establish universal activation or eligibility. Never activate an offer, assume card membership, or change the normal shopping quote price from these facts.

## Shared implemented boundary

src/domain/offers.ts exports offerLimits, offerDateSchema, offerConditionSchema, retailerOfferSchema/RetailerOffer, offerCapabilitiesSchema/OfferCapabilities, offerSourceResultSchema/OfferSourceResult, OfferReadOptions and currentOffer. src/stores/provider.ts adds optional capabilities.offers and readOffers(context, {signal?}). Existing providers need no new method until their assigned adapter card implements it.

OfferSourceResult distinguishes ready with an empty offers array from unsupported, signed-out and bounded error codes. Every result binds providerId/storeId. A ready personal result requires a private opaque accountKey matching the existing K-Ruoka account hash. This is service-only binding; do not forward it, source authentication data or loyalty IDs into inference or diagnostics. Normalized objects are strict and contain product facts only. Titles remain untrusted display text; the later task context must use selected product/condition fields rather than forwarding whole source objects.

RetailerOffer retains title, bounded product variants with stock evidence, price amount/basis/quantity/unitLabel, reference-only normalPrice, validUntil, remainingRedemptions, eligibility and fixed condition codes. Personal offers must be conditional and include plussa-required plus activation-unverified. Unknown validity, redemption count or price basis needs its matching condition. IDs must be unique in a feed, and product IDs and conditions unique in each offer. currentOffer excludes exhausted, explicitly expired and wholly unavailable/unknown-stock offers. A conditional offer is an idea candidate, never unconditional price authority.

Bounds are app policy, not claims about retailer pagination: at most 30 normalized offers, 200 raw offers, 20 variants per offer and 262,144 response bytes. An oversized/malformed source fails visibly; it is not an empty feed. Expose truncation when a bounded source is reduced to 30. No complete-source guarantee is implied by this limit. For personal reads, use the existing serialized site queue and three logical reads: existing account/store check, offers, then account/store recheck. Existing build resynchronization may retry each read once; at most six HTTP attempts. Check cancellation before and after each read, and discard results after account/store/source changes. No persistent offer cache.

## F11.5 handoff

The existing account check reads basket/active; do not call the get_cart tool, update a reviewed approval, choose a store/time, add quantities or issue basket PATCH/events. Add tests/k-ruoka-offers.test.ts for site request shapes, account/store binding, build retry, cancellation, valid/conditional/bundle/unknown units, expired/exhausted/empty/signed-out, malformed/oversized/error and zero PATCH/cart mutation cases.
