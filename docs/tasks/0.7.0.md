## F4. Prices for weighed goods and unclear packs

Today products sold by the kilo, or with an unreadable pack label, stay unresolved on both chains, so most real baskets have an incomplete total. After F4, weighed products get an approximate price from the kilo price and the needed amount, marked as approximate. An unclear pack can be confirmed once and Korikone remembers it for that product.

F4.1 and F4.3 use the in-app store sessions from U3 where a chain has one.

## F15. Resolve general groceries consistently across stores

Use one extensible category and preference system for generic requests such as milk, bread, eggs, mince, onions, rice, cream and coffee. AI extracts the requested category, quantity and explicit qualifiers; validated domain code applies saved preferences and suitability rules, then retailer adapters retrieve and normalize candidates. Product IDs, stock, prices and pack quantities always come from the retailer. Category-specific rules are data in the shared matcher, not separate UI or AI workflows for each food.

Selection order: explicit request, applicable household preference, category suitability and exclusions, then existing brand preference and lowest cost for enough packs. Plain milk without a preference admits kevytmaito, rasvaton maito and täysmaito; it excludes plant drinks, flavoured milk and cooking products. More-specific requests stay specific. If a preferred type is unavailable, explain it and offer alternatives for approval rather than silently changing type. A one-time product swap does not silently become a household preference. Name-based suitability never certifies allergens or dietary safety.

Direction agreed on 9 October 2026: improve automatic product selection using local rules, fixtures and user feedback, and expose understandable controls in Advanced Settings. After an explicit list update, offer one optional review popup grouping all uncertain rows; no per-product popups or interruption while typing. Clear choices are priced normally; dismissed or unreviewed uncertain rows remain unresolved, never silently substituted. A confirmed choice applies to the current list; it becomes a lasting preference only when the shopper explicitly selects "Muista valinta" / "Remember this". This means saved local preferences and matching rules; model training or sending additional purchase history to an external service is not authorized by this decision. Advanced Settings must allow inspecting, editing, forgetting and resetting saved choices.

Open product decisions, raised on 9 October 2026: define category boundaries and when a visible category default is sufficient versus an uncertain row needing the optional review. On 10 October 2026 the owner confirmed lowest total cost for enough whole packs; this question is settled. Decide how saved requirements differ from soft preferences and which substitutions require approval. F15.1/F15.5 must record the owner's answers and the initial category boundaries before dependent implementation; these questions do not block the fixture audit. The remember-only-on-request policy and single optional popup are settled.

The audit must also distinguish shopper-supplied qualifiers from AI-generated assumptions. Today's draft prompt asks for a meat type even when the note may only say "jauheliha"; generated specificity must not be mistaken for an explicit shopper choice. Review recipe-inferred requirements separately from household shopping preferences, and record where a correction or preference will be shown and saved. Measure unresolved rows by reason and test wrong selections as failures; a lower unresolved count alone does not demonstrate better matching.

## F16. Evaluation and one bounded planning operation

Owner addition, 10 October 2026: AI calls from v0.7.0 onward use a provider-neutral seam. F16.16/F16.17 establish it before task integration; ChatGPT stays default, while fake local/alternative providers verify extension points. F21 ships actual local connectivity later. Receipt import/enrichment is separate v0.9.0 F6 work under [receipt learning](../receipt-learning.md); F16.5 now explicitly excludes raw receipt text.

One explicit submit owns interpretation, local requirements, catalogue matching, optional candidate reasoning and atomic commit. Planning is read-only. Maximum three AI requests per operation: one interpretation, at most one existing interpretation-schema repair, at most one batched resolver. Limit/rate/provider/cancel errors are not automatically retried; resolver errors get no repair call. Catalogue concurrency/search bounds are explicit in F16.9, not unbounded UI effects.

Request coverage is an explicit acceptance gate: every reference-case requested dish/grocery maps to a resulting meal/row or a visible unresolved request, including a request with no searchable requirement. Measure omissions against independently authored fixture expectations derived from the user note, not just the model's own extracted list. An internal intent ledger can catch downstream drops but does not prove that AI understood every part of arbitrary text. Unknown quantity/category stays visible and needs correction rather than being invented or silently removed.
