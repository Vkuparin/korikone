## F11. Meal ideas from offers

The original goal of planning meals around offers. "Ideas from offers" suggests a few meals that use products on offer at the active store; the shopper adds the ones they want to the note. ChatGPT is used only when the shopper asks.

F11.4 must settle discovery and eligibility before F11.1. Public campaigns and personal offers are distinct capabilities. A newer fallback worker alone does not extend the default embedded K-Ruoka client. This finding does not block v0.7.0; no dependency upgrade or live account call is authorized by this planning update.

Owner decision, 11 October 2026: expand Korikone's local K-Ruoka client through its existing signed-in Kauppa session. Keep the fallback worker pinned and retailer transport/normalization separate from shopping policy/UI. A shared standalone client/package is a future option if a second consumer exists; maintaining a K-Ruoka MCP fork is not assigned scope.
