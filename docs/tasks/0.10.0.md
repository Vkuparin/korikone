## F11. Meal ideas from offers

The original goal of planning meals around offers. "Ideas from offers" suggests a few meals that use products on offer at the active store; the shopper adds the ones they want to the note. ChatGPT is used only when the shopper asks.

PM review, 11 October 2026: F5.2 establishes fields, not a supported store-wide offer feed. F5.3 can show S-kaupat's reported campaigns after normalization; current K-Ruoka has no offer marker. F11.4 must settle discovery and eligibility before F11.1. Public campaigns and personal offers are distinct capabilities. A newer fallback worker alone does not extend the default embedded K-Ruoka client. This finding does not block v0.7.0; no dependency upgrade or live account call is authorized by this planning update.

Owner decision, 11 October 2026: expand Korikone's local K-Ruoka client through its existing signed-in Kauppa session. F11.4 establishes the source contract; F11.5 implements the K-Ruoka boundary; F11.1 aggregates supported chain offers; F11.2/F11.3 deliver ideas and focused development evidence. Keep the fallback worker pinned and retailer transport/normalization separate from shopping policy/UI. A shared standalone client/package is a future option if a second consumer exists; maintaining a K-Ruoka MCP fork is not assigned scope.
