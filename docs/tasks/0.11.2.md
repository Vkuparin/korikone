# 0.11.2: Composer-to-cart reliability

Canonical task list for the v0.11.2 polishing patch. Read [the roadmap](../roadmap.md) and [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), then load only the assigned card and its dependencies.

Owner request, 25 October 2026: simple requests of 5–10 dishes and other items often fail after about a minute. The most important outcome is that something relevant to the request lands in the list; a partial list is always better than a complete failure after a long wait. Additions, follow-ups, modifications and changes of mind must work. Fixtures only; ChatGPT usage is limited. Live app checks on the owner's PC are authorized. Checkout and payment stay manual.

Evidence from the owner's local operation metrics (numeric, redacted): two planning failures as `invalid-output` after one repair, at 113 s and 118 s (two calls of about a minute each), and one planning failure as `storeUnavailable` after 11 successful catalogue reads at 40 s. In each case the whole request was discarded.
