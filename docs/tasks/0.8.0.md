## F12. Take the list to a phone by QR

A local plain-text snapshot, with no server/account/mobile sync or retailer-cart action. Scanning does not automatically save a list on the phone: its scanner may display text or offer copy/share, after which the user saves it in Notes or another app. Long lists require several numbered codes. Explain this limitation in the UI and keep copy/save available.
