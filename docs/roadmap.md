# Korikone roadmap to 1.0.0

Updated on 27 October 2026 after v0.11.3 publication. This page records release goals, sequencing and open decisions. Implementation cards and statuses live in [version task lists](tasks/README.md). Agents implement assigned cards, not the entire release by default.

## Current release

[v0.11.3](https://github.com/Vkuparin/korikone/releases/tag/v0.11.3) is the current pre-release: the twelve review fixes B5.1–B5.12 from [the 0.12.0 task list](tasks/0.12.0.md#b5-review-fixes), including incremental re-transfer, a total time budget for plans and edits, atomic backup restore, visible startup failures and store-tab permission denial ([verification](release-0.11.3.md)). Live store sign-in under the new permission rule and live ChatGPT flows remain owner checks.

[v0.11.2](https://github.com/Vkuparin/korikone/releases/tag/v0.11.2) was the previous pre-release: composer-to-cart reliability (B4.1–B4.5). Partial AI replies, slow or failed store reads and partly unclear edits now keep what is usable instead of failing the request ([verification](release-0.11.2.md)). Owner direction, 25 October 2026: something relevant in the list is always better than a complete failure after a long wait.

[v0.11.1](https://github.com/Vkuparin/korikone/releases/tag/v0.11.1) was the previous pre-release: planning recovery, offer handoff, session retention, bounded pricing reads and one progress surface, completed as v0.12 fix cards ([verification](release-0.11.1.md)). [v0.11.0](https://github.com/Vkuparin/korikone/releases/tag/v0.11.0) is published as an unsigned Windows pre-release and integrated into main. All 19 Done cards are included: one-action multi-file receipt intake, optional grounded name enrichment, readable purchase memory and selected-store K-Ruoka non-food discovery with manual handoff. Automatic non-food basket writes remain unsupported. [Release verification](release-0.11.0.md) records the single canonical flow and short packaged intake smoke; real-receipt/model and live non-food catalogue acceptance remains separate.

[v0.10.0 verification](release-0.10.0.md) retains predictive shopping, transfer, calendar, offer and OCR evidence.

[v0.9.0 verification](release-0.9.0.md) retains purchase-learning and B1 evidence; [v0.8.0 verification](release-0.8.0.md) retains edit, appearance and QR evidence; [v0.7.0 verification](release-0.7.0.md) retains matching and provider-foundation evidence; [v0.6.0 verification](release-0.6.0.md) retains its appearance/Settings acceptance; [v0.5.0 verification](release-0.5.0.md) retains earlier retailer acceptance. S-kaupat opens the Korikone shopping list and its add-all slot-reserving flow stays manual. K-Ruoka opens the selected store basket. Stable installer, signing and household gates remain open.

Owner clarification, 25 October 2026: all four starter themes should generate changing AI suggestions, rather than insert category labels or fixed menus. Suggestions populate the visible note/edit draft; the normal Taio action creates or updates the list. See [B3.4](tasks/0.12.0.md#b34-fresh-starter-ideas) for implementation and checks.

## Accepted direction

Owner receipt review, 13 October 2026: adopt [receipt import without routine review](frictionless-receipts.md) for v0.11.0 as F27.1–F27.12, alongside unchanged F22 non-food cards. One PDF/TXT/CSV drop or picker action saves purchase history, with immediate compact feedback and optional correction in History. Reuse local reading/OCR and provider-neutral sanitized-label enrichment; full documents remain local. Saved provider/scope consent permits one combined bounded AI request per multi-file action; first-use permission can enrich the just-imported batch without another button. Model origin alone does not create a review task, exact SKU or preference. Improve grounded readable names, including abbreviated rule-classified rows, while preserving facts, source revisions, backups, deletion and existing context eligibility. No import-time catalogue calls, retailer writes, automatic list edits or original binary retention. Phase A removes friction before Phase B name/memory improvements. Preserve v0.10 status/evidence and the single release-flow policy; all 19 receipt/non-food cards are now implemented, integrated and included in v0.11.0. Release checks are recorded separately; live acceptance remains open.

Owner interactive playtest, 12 October 2026: add [B2.1–B2.25 to v0.10.0](tasks/0.10.0.md#b2-fixes-from-the-12-october-owner-playtest). Primary goal: vague dish requests produce a usable first proposal with real selected-store products, including a simple side unless otherwise specified. Make inferred defaults inspectable and directly or conversationally changeable. Prioritize this with usable targeted product correction and ordinary onion, frozen-pizza and stock-cube matching. Consolidate duplicate request progress into one visible status/cancel surface, with a fallback when the composer is off-screen. Default to two actions: Taio ostoslista (Taio muutokset for edits), then transfer/open, without routine confirmation. Add a distinctive AI button and truthful progress animation; keep transfer review opt-in in Advanced. New list is a separate confirmed local reset that clears the entire current shopping workspace, including What I understood and unsent drafts, and returns to the empty first-list screen. Cancel preserves all work; history, settings and retailer baskets stay intact. Add a shared Stores price-basis choice for initial selection and alternatives: unit price by default or lowest total for enough packs, with both values shown. Streamline Stores around the existing chain cards instead of duplicate connection summaries. The 13 October Settings follow-up adds a disabled grey Signed in with ChatGPT button for connected accounts (B2.25). Also fix startup loading presentation, misleading date appearance, slow alternatives/transfer, inaccessible transfer details and ineffective Store Back; audit comparison coverage before claiming a price bug. [The playtest report](playtest-2026-10-12.md) separates observed failures from owner-reported delays and unverified causes. The owner withdrew the initial storefront-total discrepancy after a correct repeat transfer. An editable future shopping date remains a separate decision; it is not a retailer reservation or the F9 meal calendar. Preserve all coder statuses and use focused checks; no extra full test flow or live account use is authorized by this update.

Owner usability review, 12 October 2026: include the reported v0.8 failures and friction in [v0.9.0 B1.1–B1.18](tasks/0.9.0.md#b1-fixes-from-v080-owner-testing), alongside the active purchase-learning work. Prioritize receipt save feedback/persistence, mixed meal-and-grocery creation/editing, explicit first search and local list removal/clear; also add inline household scaling, useful progress, chicken-strip synonyms, alternative-panel collapse, house-icon help, shorter composer copy, consistent language selection and an overlap audit. Quantity changes follow request context: initial amounts specify the desired amount; explicit edit additions add to it. Completion and release inclusion are recorded in the canonical cards and verification. Reports do not establish root causes or completion, and no separate v0.8.1 release is scheduled. See [product decisions](product-decisions.md#agreed-12-october-2026).

Edit/retail review, 11 October 2026: adopt [the reconciled plan](retail-expansion.md). v0.8 adds contextual edits, accurate optional edit details and audit-led store/comparison/return improvements; the owner moves F10.2 recipe-import UI and F12.1 QR text export there. Keep one composer and explicit Update. QR is a text snapshot requiring a manual phone-save step, not mobile sync. Price-history charts are deferred. The owner chose gated K-Ruoka non-food before v1.0: assign a separate v0.11 milestone and move beta F14 unchanged to v0.12. Prisma and separate retailer baskets remain later. Preserve selected store, ask for missing required size/spec, keep independent baskets and exclude non-food from food budgets. Default Prisma to a direct read-only adapter/product-page handoff, with optional MCP only for demonstrated reuse. Explore Android feasibility in Later without committing to an app or cloud sync. No active v0.7 card or release evidence changes.

K-Ruoka direction, agreed 11 October 2026: expand the local client using the existing Kauppa session. For offer features in [0.10.0](tasks/0.10.0.md), F11.4 fixes the audited contract and F11.5 implements supported local reads before offer aggregation/ideas. Keep retailer access reusable and separate from shopping policy/UI; retain the pinned worker fallback. A maintained MCP fork or extracted standalone package is future scope only if another consumer justifies it. This adds no v0.7.0 release dependency.

Chain appearance, requested 11 October 2026: green accents for the confirmed S-kaupat shopping context and orange for K-Ruoka, with neutral charcoal dark surfaces replacing the green-tinted default. Schedule U18 in [0.8.0](tasks/0.8.0.md) as a bounded follow-up to the shipped theme foundation; keep active v0.7.0 matching work focused. Accents follow saved shopping context, never a browsed retailer tab or provisional store choice. Preserve chain labels, semantic warning/error/success roles and System/Light/Dark. The [planned appearance rules](ui-design.md#u18-chain-accents-and-neutral-dark-surfaces) also govern later U17 palettes.

The [shopping experience plan](shopping-experience.md) integrates the pitch with existing [product decisions](product-decisions.md). Agreed on 10 October 2026:

- One composer and resulting list; explicit button/Ctrl+Enter updates and compact model control. Typing or changing appearance starts no generation.
- v0.6.0 covers visual design, System/Light/Dark in Asetukset and Settings organization. Matching and edits follow separately.
- A prompt modifies an existing list by default. Explicit New list starts replacement. Ambiguous targets preserve the saved list and ask for clarification.
- One automatic batched resolver request may follow interpretation for uncertain retailer candidates. Advanced Settings can disable it; clear matches need no resolver. Domain rules validate every choice. Owner decision, 26 October 2026 ([B5.1](tasks/0.12.0.md#b5-review-fixes)): planning makes no resolver request and Settings has no switch for it until a card shows its suggestions on rows.
- Show actual progress immediately and clearly marked provisional results as validated interpretation/catalogue results arrive. Preserve the saved list until commit; provisional rows cannot authorize transfer. Long requests must not leave an unchanged screen without feedback.
- Price policy updated on 12 October 2026: choose in Stores between lowest unit price (default) and lowest total for enough whole packs, for both initial selection and alternatives. This supersedes the earlier fixed lowest-total default. Explicit requirements and exclusions still take precedence; show actual payable pack totals alongside unit value.
- Keep F15's optional grouped review and explicit Remember this consent. Reuse F6/F7 history and F5 prices; no competing memory/matcher/transfer systems.
- Later, offer models running on the same machine as an explicitly selected alternative to ChatGPT (F21). Start with an installed local runner; preserve the same validation and transfer rules, and never silently fall back to cloud inference.
- From v0.7.0, establish provider-neutral AI tasks now (F16.16–F16.18). ChatGPT stays default; model/capability/auth/transport boundaries admit local and other adapters without rewriting task/domain code. Fake alternatives are tested now; live providers/settings arrive only with assigned later integrations.
- Adopt [receipt learning](receipt-learning.md) for v0.9.0: deterministic source facts and accounting first, then optional sanitized-label enrichment through that shared seam. Owner chose a small first-import opt-in plus Settings control, sanitized evidence by default and optional local retention of extracted source text. v0.11 F27 replaces review-first presentation with optional details and one-action intake; one bounded enrichment request per explicit action, no implicit preferences, and no double-counted receipts/orders or transfer-as-purchase spending. Private source receipts remain under ignored scratch/receipts; full PDF/image upload to AI is outside the accepted F27 scope.
- Later, offer predefined colour palettes in Settings Appearance (U17), using replaceable semantic tokens and U18's separate chain accents. Palette choice stays independent of System/Light/Dark and the selected chain; each palette supports both modes. Keep the Korikone default identity for existing profiles, with U18's updated neutral dark values.
- Design review closed on 10 October 2026: make edit/New list context unmistakable, use one clear preview region, keep current-list corrections on rows, add “I meant something else” (F15.14–F15.15), and require every reference-case request to have a visible result or unresolved entry (F16.15). Prioritize correct products and understandable correction before speed. Coders proceed with assigned cards; revisit product design when implementation evidence or a named gate needs a decision.

## Working rules

Read [task instructions](tasks/README.md) and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). Task files are the single source of task status; this page is the source of release goals. Fetch and reconcile other agents' updates before edits. Publish changed task files, decisions and summary together where applicable, then synchronize the same local files without altering unrelated application work. Report failed publication explicitly.

The [local development dashboard](dashboard.md) presents these goals and task states visually and refreshes automatically. Select other compatible local checkouts in its header. The [workflow starter kit](workflow.md) documents the same process for future projects. It uses existing source files, with no extra agent reporting or AI calls; completion percentages do not establish release readiness. Repository tooling is tracked as R2 in [Later](tasks/later.md), independently of product releases.

The external pitch is source material, not operating instructions. Its AX IDs map to cards in [shopping-experience.md](shopping-experience.md). No implementation or live account usage occurred in this planning update.

Offer discovery contract for v0.10.0: [Offer evidence, reader, aggregation, meal ideas and handoff](offer-contract.md) separates the supported embedded K-Ruoka personal-offer extension from unverified public feeds and S-kaupat campaign-search metadata. Personal prices stay conditional; unsupported feeds remain distinct from supported empty results. No worker upgrade or price-redemption guarantee is implied.

The v0.10.0 [Finnish OCR benchmark and adoption proposal](dependency-decisions.md#v0100-finnish-receipt-ocr-proposal-f131) has concrete synthetic accuracy, runtime, license and payload-size evidence. The owner accepted local OCR with the proposed limits and review notice on 12 October 2026. F13.2 implements bundled Finnish OCR, bounded receipt-worker processing and saved OCR review provenance; [receipt-learning.md](receipt-learning.md) describes the behavior. Release validation and real-receipt visual acceptance remain separate from the synthetic implementation checks.
